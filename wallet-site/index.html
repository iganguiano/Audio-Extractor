Questions? Answers.

Will it hold one or two cards securely?

Yes. The band keeps tension on the plates even when they're almost touching, so a single card stays put.

Can I still tap to pay?

The plates block contactless signals while cards are inside. Push your card up with your thumb, tap, and slide it back.

How do I carry cash?

Fold bills and slip them under the strap on the back. If you carry a lot of cash, swap the strap for the included money clip using the T6 driver.

Does carbon fiber scratch?

The matte finish hides everyday wear well. It won't dent like aluminum, and it won't mark your phone or keys.

What does the warranty cover?

Plates, frame, screws and band. If anything breaks under normal use, we replace the part or the wallet.
