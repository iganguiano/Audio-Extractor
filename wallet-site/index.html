Engineered to be forgotten in your pocket.

Every part of the K1 is there for a reason. The rest was machined away.

3K twill carbon plates

Real carbon fiber, not a printed wrap. Stiffer than aluminum at a fraction of the weight, and it won't scratch your phone.

RFID blocking

The plates shield contactless cards from unauthorised scans at 13.56 MHz. Pull a card out and it taps to pay as normal.

Expanding elastic band

A woven band holds the plates together and stretches from one card to twelve without loosening over time.

Cash strap

The contoured strap on the back holds folded bills or receipts securely. Swap it for the included money clip if you prefer.

Replaceable hardware

Ten stainless hex screws hold it together. A T6 driver comes in the box, so you can swap the band or plates for life.

Thumb-push access

The cut-out at the base lets you push your stack up and fan it with one thumb. No digging, no pulling cards out one by one.

Daily use

From pocket to payment in one motion.

  1. Load your cards

    Slide up to twelve cards between the plates. The band adjusts to the stack on its own.

  2. Push with your thumb

    Press through the cut-out at the base and the stack rises out of the top.

  3. Fan and pick

    Cards stagger as they rise, so the one you need is easy to grab.

Questions

Before you switch.

Will it hold my cards securely with only one or two inside?

Yes. The band keeps tension on the plates even when they're almost touching, so a single card stays put.

Can I still tap to pay?

The plates block contactless signals while cards are inside. Push your card up with your thumb, tap, and slide it back.

How do I carry cash?

Fold bills in half or thirds and slip them under the strap on the back. If you carry a lot of cash, swap the strap for the included money clip using the T6 driver.

Does carbon fiber scratch?

The matte finish hides everyday wear well. It won't dent like aluminum, and it won't mark your phone or keys.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

Plates, frame, screws and band. If anything breaks under normal use, we replace the part or the wallet.
