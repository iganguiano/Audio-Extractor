A jacket pocket with no bulge. The Halfstack slides in flat and comes out with one hand.

Cards sit edge to edge between the plates, so the stack stays as wide as one card. A thumb on the cut-out pushes them up when you pay.

The parts of a Halfstack. Plates, clip, band and edges, each doing one job.

  • Aluminium plates

    Twelve cards and folded notes fit between two plates thin enough to pinch.

  • Steel cash clip

    Notes folded once slide under the clip on the back, and the band crosses them.

  • RFID shield

    The metal plates block contactless scans of the cards inside.

  • Chamfered edges

    Each plate is CNC-cut on both sides, so no corner catches a hand or a card.

Why a Halfstack

Questions about the Halfstack

Does the band hold a single card?

Yes. The elastic band keeps the plates pressed together, so one card stays put.

Can I tap a card to pay?

Push the card up through the cut-out and tap it. Inside the plates, contactless scans are blocked.

How many notes fit under the cash clip?

A few notes folded once. The band crosses them so they stay flat.

What does the warranty cover?

The plates, frame, clip and band, for the life of the wallet.
